**Film or TV Review**

**Task:** Choose a film or TV series you particularly like and write a review. Try to compare the film with similar movies you may have seen.

**The things to think about when analysing a film are the following:**

*   **Genre** - drama, comedy, sci-fi, western, film noir, horror, action...
*   **Characters** - are they likeable or believable? Do they compare to similar characters you may have seen?
*   **Themes and Sub-themes** - there is usually a main theme in a film and (sometimes) a sub-theme. For example, a film such as *Doctor Zhivago* (1965), presented the main theme of a love story against the background – in this case the sub-theme of the Russian Revolution.
*   **Plot** - the story line of the film
*   **Setting** - Geographic (where it is set – town or city); historic (what period of history, if at all); social (where in society do the characters operate? For example, working class industrial, middle-class suburban, aristocratic etc).
*   **Cinematography** - motion picture photography. How is it filmed? For example, were rich colours used or subdued colour? Was it made in black and white for a special mood? Was it grainy to create a mood or a sense of history? Was the camera hand-held for effect, as in the thick of a battle scene, or were the shots very steady and epic-like.
*   **Editing** - how is the film paced? Is it too fast, with too many quick, distracting cuts, too slow and ponderous, carefully set to music?
*   **Music** - theme and incidental. There is usually a main theme to a movie - such as that in *Star Wars* for example. The incidental music is that which is played throughout the film for mood and emotional, dramatic or comic effect.
*   **Irony** - a situation that turns out to be the opposite of what the audience expects, given certain circumstances.
*   **Satire** - often used in comedy to make fun of institutions, situations or individuals.
*   **Direction of the film** - was it well-directed overall? Why why not?
*   **Special effects (if any)** - how well were they done? How were they made? Were they like other effects you may have seen?
*   **Costumes** - did they represent a particular historic period, uniforms or futuristic, science fiction types of clothing.
*   **Makeup** - how effective was this? How creative and believable.
*   **Film sets, backlots** - did the film company build spectacular or convincing streets, building frontages, imaginary worlds right there at the studio premises or somewhere in the countryside? Many major film studios like Universal, Paramount and Fox have large outdoor areas where they build streets or various structures.
